## Tiny Swords, by Pixel Frog — CC0 1.0

Units (`units/`), terrain (`terrain/`), decorations (`deco/`), effects (`effects/`),
resources (`resources/`), interface frames, ribbons and buttons (`ui/`), and
`buildings/Tower_Purple.png` and `buildings/House_Purple.png` are from

dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 (`LICENSE-CC0.txt`). Copied unmodified from
the copy bundled in Agent Quest; `deco/Deco_NN.png` is the pack's `Deco/NN.png`.

## Building art, from Agent Quest — MIT

`buildings/Library.png`, `Forge.png`, `Arena.png`, `Tower.png` and `Castle.png` are the
custom building art from [FulAppiOS/Agent-Quest](https://github.com/FulAppiOS/Agent-Quest)
(`client/public/assets/themes/tiny-swords-cc0/BuildingsCustom/`), used under that
project's MIT license. Its copyright notice is kept in `LICENSE-agent-quest-MIT.txt`, as
the license requires.

## The guild's own props — MIT, with this project

`../props/` (bookshelves, books, scrolls, a lectern and a crystal orb) are drawn for
agent-guild in the Tiny Swords style by `scripts/art/props.py`, which regenerates them.
They are part of this project and under its license.
